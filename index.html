From Curitiba to Charlotte

I grew up in Curitiba, in the south of Brazil, and I've been cooking since I was nine years old, standing on a chair next to my mom.

I came to Charlotte in 2007. For years, friends asked where they could get real coxinhas and a proper bolo de aniversário. In September 2022 we opened our doors on Johnston Road to answer that question.

We're a family business. Everything is made here, the way we make it at home. Come say oi.

— Marcia
